# Abib Temidayo Olatunde

![](/images/abib-olatunde.jpg)

I obtained my bachelor’s degree in chemistry from Lagos State University, Nigeria, followed by a master’s degree in chemistry from the University of Antwerp, Belgium.
My current research focus lies at the intersection of physical chemistry and data-driven modeling.
I am particularly interested in understanding how molecular structure and formulation parameters influence physicochemical properties such as vapor pressure and interfacial phenomena.
More broadly, I aim to integrate high-throughput experimentation with machine learning to develop predictive frameworks for complex systems.
