# Pim Dankloff

![](/images/pim-dankloff.jpg)

I’m Pim Dankloff, a 3rd-year PhD candidate at Radboud University.
In my research, I focus on active learning and high-throughput experimentation in surfactant chemistry.
I developed a self-driving platform where the surface tension of surfactant solutions is measured using pendant drop tensiometry.
Currently, I am trying to leverage this platform to uncover cool new chemistry in binary surfactant mixtures.
In my free time, I like to go to concerts or play the guitar!
